‘Message d'Amour’

‘Nothing carries the root of its being in

Itself’, so said the philosopher. Hollow

Was I, then love came to me. Seeing in

Your adorable eyes that I had to follow

To reach experience’s blessed pinnacle.

With your love the deepest unities I see.

Subject and object, wisdom rabbinical.

The harmony of all in one. Within unity

And disunity joyful dynamism. Freedom

With absolute life in nature. Distinction

And difference. All exchange a te deum.

The actual, the possible, and recognition.

At the heart of all my understanding, my dove,

Are you, to whom I send this message of love.
