![](/uploads/beckett%203.webp)BECKETT: LIEBEN 2026

17–20 September 2026 — Liverpool.  Lieben.

Presented by Unreal Cities + Institute of Irish Studies, University of Liverpool

It's the 3rd edition of the biennial Beckett festival. After Beckett: Confined (2022) and Beckett: Unbound (2024), this year is called Lieben [German for "to love"] and it’s all about Beckett’s![](/uploads/beckett%202.webp)love for German expressionism.

He first got into it in the 1930s through the Sinclair family in Kassel — the painting is Peggy Sinclair by Karl Leyhausen (1931), Beckett's cousin and early love.![](/uploads/bckett%205.webp)
